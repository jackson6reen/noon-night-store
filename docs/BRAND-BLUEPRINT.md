## Unique mechanism hooks

- **Negative hook (primary cause): Gut-Clock Drift.** Digestion and sleep run on linked daily rhythms. Late meals, stress and short sleep pull them apart, and each side drags the other further out of sync: food ferments by day, and an unsettled gut keeps the body on alert by night.
- **Positive hook (secret sauce): Day-Night Sync.** Support digestion during the day, when the gut is meant to work, and help the body power down at night. Done together every day, the two clocks start pulling the same way again.

## Compliance guardrails (important for supplements in the U.S.)

- Only **structure/function** claims ("supports", "helps relieve occasional"). Never "cures", "treats", "prevents" or a named disease (IBS, insomnia).
- Every claim with `*` needs the FDA disclaimer (already in the footer and on product pages).
- FTC: testimonials must be real and typical, and any paid or free-product reviews must be disclosed.
- Meta/Google ads: no before/after body shots, no "you" + health condition targeting ("Are you bloated?" in ads can get rejected; it's fine on the site).
