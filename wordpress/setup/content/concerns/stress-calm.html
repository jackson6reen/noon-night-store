"Wired but tired" is a real pattern

When stress stays high all day, your body keeps the "go" signals running: elevated cortisol, tense muscles, a busy brain. By bedtime you're exhausted but can't switch off, then you wake up unrefreshed and do it again. Breaking the loop means supporting your body during the day, not just at bedtime.

Daytime: ashwagandha (KSM-66)

Ashwagandha is an adaptogen, an herb traditionally used to help the body handle stress. KSM-66 is a full-spectrum root extract used in several clinical studies on stress, cortisol and sleep quality in adults.* See Noon Steady.

Evening: magnesium glycinate

Magnesium supports normal nervous-system and muscle function, and stress can increase how fast your body uses it. A nightly dose of glycinate is a gentle way to unwind.* See Night Calm.

Day-to-night calm: Noon Steady with lunch, Night Calm before bed. If sleep is the bigger problem, see our sleep guide.

Three free tools that work

  • Physiological sigh: two quick inhales through the nose, one long exhale through the mouth. Repeat 3–5 times.
  • A hard stop: pick a time after which work email stays closed.
  • Daylight before 10 a.m.: ten minutes outside helps set your body clock for a better night.

Supplements are support, not treatment

If stress, worry or low mood is affecting your daily life for more than a couple of weeks, please talk to a doctor or a licensed therapist. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
