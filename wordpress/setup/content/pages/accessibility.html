[nn_business field="business_name"] wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to shop and read our site with ease.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. This is an ongoing effort.

What we've done

  • Keyboard navigation for the whole site, with a "Skip to content" link and visible focus outlines.
  • Text and buttons with strong color contrast, and text that resizes without breaking the layout.
  • Descriptive labels on form fields, buttons and icons for screen readers.
  • Product information (ingredients, directions, warnings) as real text, not just images.
  • FAQ sections that work with keyboards and screen readers, without depending on scripts.
  • Respect for your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • A mobile layout that works at 320 px wide and when zoomed to 200%.

Known limitations

Some third-party tools (such as payment forms or embedded content) are provided by other companies. We choose accessible providers where we can, but we don't fully control them.

Need help or found a barrier?

If anything on our site is hard to use, please tell us. We'll help you place your order another way and fix the problem.
