This Privacy Policy explains how [nn_business field="legal_entity"] ("[nn_business field="business_name"]", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit our website or buy from us, and the rights you have over it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number, order details, and messages you send to support.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe (and, if offered, PayPal). Your full card number is sent directly to the payment processor and is never stored on our servers.
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring site, collected with cookies and similar technologies. Analytics and advertising cookies are used only if you agree.
  • Health-related information: we don't ask for medical information. If you choose to share health details with support (for example, when asking whether a product suits you), we use them only to answer you, and we never sell or share them for advertising.

2. How we use information

  • To process, ship and support your orders, including sending order data to our fulfillment partner who packs and ships your products.
  • To answer questions and handle refunds.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve our site and, with your consent, measure and personalize ads.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our site secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the store: payment processors (Stripe), our fulfillment and shipping partners, website hosting, email delivery, customer support tools and, with your consent, analytics and advertising partners such as Google and Meta. They may use it only to provide services to us.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some state laws, letting advertising partners use cookies for targeted ads can count as "selling" or "sharing." We only do this with your consent, and you can opt out at any time (see section 5).

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the cart and checkout. With your permission, we also use analytics cookies (to understand how the site is used) and marketing cookies (to measure and personalize ads). You can change your choice at any time with the Your Privacy Choices link in our footer.

5. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect and how we use it, and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information;
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

How to opt out: use the Your Privacy Choices link. We also honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal: if your browser sends it, we automatically treat it as an opt-out of sale and sharing.

To make any other request, email [nn_business field="support_email"]. We'll verify your request by matching information we already have, and respond within the time the law requires (usually 45 days). You can use an authorized agent. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

6. Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal purposes (typically up to 7 years), and other information only as long as necessary for the purposes above.

7. Security

We use encryption (HTTPS), a PCI-compliant payment processor, limited staff access and regular software updates to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, so please contact us right away if you suspect a problem.

8. Children

Our site and products are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
