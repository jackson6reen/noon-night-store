Not medical advice

Everything on this website, including product pages, articles and emails, is for general information and education. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with your doctor or another qualified healthcare professional.

Talk to your doctor first if you:

  • are pregnant, nursing or trying to conceive;
  • take any prescription or over-the-counter medication, including antidepressants, blood thinners, hormonal birth control or hormone therapy, sedatives, thyroid, diabetes or blood-pressure medication;
  • have a medical condition, including liver, kidney, thyroid, autoimmune or hormone-sensitive conditions;
  • are scheduled for surgery;
  • are under 18.

Read every label

Each product page has a Safety tab with specific warnings. Always follow the directions and warnings on the product label, and don't exceed the recommended dose. Stop using a product and contact your doctor if you have a reaction.

See a doctor when symptoms are serious

Supplements support everyday wellbeing. They're not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment. Please seek medical care for severe, sudden, persistent or worsening symptoms, such as period pain that disrupts daily life, very heavy bleeding, ongoing insomnia, persistent bloating, unexplained weight loss or blood in your stool.

Results vary

Individual results vary. Reviews and stories shared on our site reflect individual experiences and are not a guarantee of results.

In an emergency, call 911. If you're in emotional crisis in the US, call or text 988.
